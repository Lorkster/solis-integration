Connect your Solis inverter

You need a SolisCloud API key.

  1. Log in to soliscloud.com in a web browser.
  2. Open Account → Basic Settings → API Management and activate the API. Copy the Key ID and Key Secret.
  3. To let the app change the battery schedule, ask Solis support (a ticket at solis-service.solisinverters.com) to enable control access for your inverter.

The app starts in Monitor only: it shows prices and plans but does not change anything on the inverter until you switch to Automatic.
